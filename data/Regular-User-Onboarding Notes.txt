Do NOT place passwords inside profile/person data.

Do NOT add KYC fields.


The result should be a clean, extensible Regular User onboarding architecture that can later support Institutional Users without needing to rewrite the entire registration system.

### Recommended implementation order

For your current Roots project, I would make the files work in this order:

**`onboarding.html` → `onboarding.js` → `registration-data.js` → `validation.js` → `store.js` → `data.js` → existing `tree.js`**

The important architectural point is that **registration creates the person identity, while the Family Tree adds genealogy**. Your existing `tree.html` already separates the person's identity/profile from kinship and family relationships, which is exactly the separation we want to preserve. 

I would make the next piece either **the exact `registration-data.js` country/phone/network/location schema** or **the complete `onboarding.html` UI**. Which should come first?
